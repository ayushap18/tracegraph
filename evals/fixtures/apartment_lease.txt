Residential lease agreement (summary of terms)

Landlord: Harbourview Properties Ltd. Tenant: Sam Okafor.
Property: Flat 4B, 17 Canal Street, Manchester.

Term: the lease starts on 1 September 2025 and ends on 31 August 2026.
Rent: 1,450 pounds per month, due on the 1st of each month. A late fee of 50 pounds applies after the 5th.
Deposit: 2,900 pounds (two months of rent), held in a government-approved deposit scheme.
Notice: either party must give at least 60 days written notice to end the lease early.
Pets: one cat or small dog is allowed with a one-off pet fee of 300 pounds. No other animals.
Utilities: the tenant pays electricity, gas and internet. Water is included in the rent.
Repairs: the landlord fixes structural problems and appliances within 14 days of being told.
Subletting is not allowed without the landlord's written consent.
